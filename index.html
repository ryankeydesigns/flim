对称构图Symmetrical Composition Napoleon Dynamite (2004)

This scene uses symmetrical composition. The character is placed in the center of the frame, and the hallway on the left and right sides looks almost identical. This creates a balanced and organized visual effect. The symmetrical composition draws the viewer’s attention to the character and also emphasizes his lonely and awkward feeling in the school environment.

对称构图Symmetrical Composition (Jurassic World)

In the film Jurassic World, this shot uses symmetrical composition. The character (played by Chris Pratt) is placed in the center, aligning with the vertical axis, which draws attention to his facial expression and highlights tension and focus.The repeated structures in the background strengthen the sense of symmetry and stability, while the slightly off-center hand breaks the symmetry, adding movement and tension. This composition emphasizes the balance and conflict between the human and the dinosaur.

对称构图Symmetrical Composition(JOKER)
In the film Joker, this shot also uses symmetrical composition. The stairs and handrails create strong leading lines and near symmetry, guiding the viewer’s eyes toward the character (played by Joaquin Phoenix).Although the character is slightly off-center, his raised arms balance the frame visually. The symmetry of the background contrasts with his dynamic pose, emphasizing his transformation and chaotic emotions.

对称构图Symmetrical Composition

In the TV series Sherlock, this shot uses strong symmetrical composition. The character (played by Benedict Cumberbatch) stands exactly in the center, aligned with the vertical axis.The two windows on both sides create a clear mirror-like balance, enhancing symmetry and drawing attention to the character. The perspective lines also lead toward the center, making him the visual focus.This composition emphasizes order, intelligence, and control, reflecting the character’s logical and analytical mind.

顶视构图Bird’s-eye / Overhead / Top-down (Inception)

This image is from Inception, directed by Christopher Nolan.It uses a bird’s-eye (top-down) shot to show characters lying in a dream-like space surrounded by scattered objects, emphasizing disorientation, psychological depth, and the blurred line between reality and dreams while visually organizing the scene from above.

顶视构图Bird’s-eye / Overhead / Top-down（The Avengers)

This scene is from The Avengers. It uses a bird’s-eye view (overhead shot), where the camera looks down at the characters from above, making them appear smaller while clearly showing the surrounding environment and the aftermath of the battle.This type of composition enhances the sense of space and scale, helping the audience understand the setting and the characters’ positions. In addition, the characters looking upward increases tension and suggests that something important or dangerous is about to happen, adding more dramatic impact to the scene.

引导线构图Leading Lines（The Dark Knight）

In The Dark Knight, the horizontal and vertical elements in the environment—such as ceiling lights, walls, and table edges—form subtle leading lines that guide the viewer’s eye directly toward the key piece of evidence in the character’s hand, reinforcing both the narrative focus and the tension of the scene.

 引导线构图Leading Lines（Rear Window）

In Rear Window, the director combines the rule of thirds with strong spatial lines created by windows, furniture, and interior architecture, allowing the viewer’s gaze to move naturally across the frame while emphasizing the relationship and interaction between the characters.

引导线构图Leading Lines（The Graduate）

In The Graduate, the use of foreground framing and diagonal lines—formed by the surrounding environment and figures—creates powerful leading lines that draw the viewer’s attention to the central character, enhancing both the visual focus and the underlying sense of humor and tension in the scene.
